# Air Hockey

Air Hockey.

## Highlights
- PvP and CPU modes (Easy / Medium / Hard)
- Power‑ups: speed boost, shield, multiball, paddle grow/shrink
- Time Attack and Sudden Death
- Customizable board themes and puck/paddle styles
- Haptics and sound toggles

## Controls
- Drag your paddle within your half of the board.

## Tech
- Swift + SpriteKit
- iOS orientation handling (portrait menus, landscape gameplay)
